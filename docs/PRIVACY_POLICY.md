# LensMood Privacy Policy

_Effective date: [SET ON PUBLICATION] · Applies to LensMood for iOS,
including its Share-Sheet extension and Home/Lock Screen widgets._

LensMood is a camera and photo-developing app. The short version: **your
photos never leave your phone, and we collect nothing.**

## What LensMood does with your photos

- Every photograph and video you capture, import, or develop is processed
  **entirely on your device**, using Apple's on-device frameworks (Core
  Image, Metal, Vision, AVFoundation).
- Photos and videos are **never uploaded** anywhere. LensMood has no
  server, no cloud processing, and no account system.
- Developed photos are stored in the app's private on-device library and,
  only when you tap save or share, written to your Photos library or handed
  to the iOS share sheet.
- Deleting a photo in LensMood, or deleting the app, removes the app's
  copies from your device. We could not recover them if we wanted to —
  we never had them.

## Data collection

**We do not collect any data.** LensMood has:

- **No analytics or telemetry.** Nothing about your usage is measured,
  recorded, or transmitted — the app ships with zero networking of its own.
- **No tracking.** No advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting, no
  App Tracking Transparency prompt — because there is nothing to track.
- **No accounts.** You never sign in, and we never learn your name, email
  address, or anything else about you.
- **No third-party SDKs** other than Apple's own frameworks. Purchases, if
  you make one, go through Apple's StoreKit.

The App Privacy label for LensMood on the App Store is **"Data Not
Collected."**

## Device permissions LensMood asks for

| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | To take photographs and record tapes inside the app. | Only when you open the in-app camera or Tape screen. |
| Microphone | To record sound with tapes (video). | Only when you record a tape. |
| Photo library — add only | To save your developed photos and tapes. | Only when you tap save. LensMood requests **add-only** access; it cannot browse your library. Importing uses Apple's system photo picker, which shows the app only the photos you explicitly choose. |

You can decline or revoke any of these in iOS Settings; the rest of the
app keeps working.

## Purchases

If LensMood offers a paid membership, the purchase is handled by Apple
through your Apple ID. We receive no payment details and no personal
information. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction. Purchase
state (whether the membership is owned) is checked on-device via StoreKit.

## Children

LensMood does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If LensMood's behavior ever changes (for example, if an optional online
feature is added), this policy will be updated before that version ships,
with the effective date above revised.
