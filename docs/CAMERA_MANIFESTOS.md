# Camera personality manifestos

These specifications describe authored behavior, not claims of measured simulation.
Internal engine IDs remain stable for fixture continuity; public names may change after
legal review.

## Disposable (`disposable`)

- Protects: recognisable faces and the warmth of shared indoor light.
- Sacrifices: even illumination, perfect corners, and clean shadows.
- Exposure: modest face lift with inconsistent background falloff.
- Color: warm flash and slightly imperfect neutralisation.
- Grain/optics: soft, visible grain; mild bloom; plastic-lens corners.
- Excels: friends after dark and candid close groups.
- Failure character: distant subjects stay dark and bright flash areas may bloom.

## Direct Flash (`iphone-flash`)

- Protects: the nearest face, crisp detail, and cool white flash.
- Sacrifices: background shadow detail and atmospheric warmth.
- Exposure: strong subject priority with deliberately fast falloff.
- Color: clean, slightly cool, glossy digital response.
- Grain/optics: little grain, little vignette, hard microcontrast.
- Excels: direct night portraits.
- Failure character: reflective skin and objects may clip before the background opens.

## Tape 94 (`camcorder-90s`)

- Protects: continuous visibility and the feeling of recorded time.
- Sacrifices: chroma precision, fine detail, and stable exposure.
- Exposure: slow auto-gain breathing, never aggressive black cutouts.
- Color: muted tube color with gentle channel contamination.
- Grain/optics: crawling luminance noise, soft edges, restrained vignette.
- Excels: moving memories and domestic interiors.
- Failure character: low light becomes noisy and smeared, not intermittently invisible.

## Street 35 (`leica-street`)

- Protects: texture, available-light relationships, and highlight structure.
- Sacrifices: cosmetic smoothing and exaggerated saturation.
- Exposure: restrained metering with gentle face awareness.
- Color: honest neutrals and quiet separation.
- Grain/optics: fine grain and subtle corner falloff.
- Excels: candid street work and window light.
- Failure character: difficult light remains difficult instead of becoming glossy.

## Editorial Strobe (`gq-editorial`)

- Protects: face structure, fabric detail, and controlled skin color.
- Sacrifices: ambient mess and casual softness.
- Exposure: shaped strobe contrast with held facial highlights.
- Color: polished but not plastic; restrained saturation.
- Grain/optics: very fine grain, clean optics.
- Excels: composed portraits and fashion-like scenes.
- Failure character: unstructured snapshots can feel severe.

## Independent Still (`a24-still`)

- Protects: atmosphere, highlight rolloff, and environmental color.
- Sacrifices: punch and immediate social-media brightness.
- Exposure: slight underexposure with open narrative shadows.
- Color: muted, scene-respectful, gently warm when the light asks for it.
- Grain/optics: soft bloom and moderate fine grain.
- Excels: quiet frames and mixed available light.
- Failure character: flat scenes stay understated.

## Noir (`film-noir`)

- Protects: facial geometry, tonal separation, and directional light.
- Sacrifices: all color and most weak shadow information.
- Exposure: allows blacks to fall while keeping meaningful highlights.
- Color: strictly monochrome after every processing stage.
- Grain/optics: visible fine grain and deliberate edge falloff.
- Excels: graphic light and expressive faces.
- Failure character: flat lighting becomes stark rather than decorative gray.

## Pocket 2002 (`y2k-digicam`)

- Protects: face visibility and immediate bright color.
- Sacrifices: highlight smoothness and subtle ambience.
- Exposure: eager auto exposure plus hard close flash.
- Color: glossy, saturated, slightly synthetic digital response.
- Grain/optics: crisp small-sensor detail with minimal film grain.
- Excels: chaotic nights and close social frames.
- Failure character: highlights clip and skin can look intentionally over-flashed.

## Instant 600 (`polaroid`)

- Protects: emotional warmth and midtone faces.
- Sacrifices: deep dynamic range and exact color neutrality.
- Exposure: compresses extremes into the printable middle.
- Color: creamy warmth and gently faded chroma.
- Grain/optics: soft chemical edge, mild vignette, restrained grain.
- Excels: tender daylight and simple portraits.
- Failure character: extreme contrast compresses rather than becoming clinically recovered.

## 8mm Home Movie (`super-8`)

- Protects: warmth, movement, and memory-like highlights.
- Sacrifices: edge cleanliness and static precision.
- Exposure: favors the bright lived moment over shadow recovery.
- Color: warm stock response with softened blues.
- Grain/optics: leading grain, gate-edge falloff, gentle glow.
- Excels: sunlit family scenes.
- Failure character: underexposure becomes dense and grain-forward.

## Toy Color (`lomo`)

- Protects: surprise and bold central color.
- Sacrifices: corner detail, neutrality, and predictable exposure.
- Exposure: lightly adaptive but intentionally imperfect.
- Color: exaggerated chroma and strong hue separation.
- Grain/optics: dark corners, visible aberration, plastic-lens softness.
- Excels: playful daylight and graphic subjects.
- Failure character: already saturated scenes can become excessive by design.

## Slide 64 (`kodachrome`)

- Protects: dense reds, daylight color relationships, and clean shadows.
- Sacrifices: latitude in extreme highlights.
- Exposure: careful, slightly conservative slide-film bias.
- Color: rich without broad saturation inflation.
- Grain/optics: fine grain and clean optics.
- Excels: daylight color and travel frames.
- Failure character: missed exposure remains visible.

## Monitor 02 (`security-cam`)

- Protects: evidence and continuous subject visibility.
- Sacrifices: flattering color, smooth noise, and cinematic composition.
- Exposure: aggressive auto gain based on the whole frame.
- Color: collapsed surveillance green with weak chroma.
- Grain/optics: coarse noise, utilitarian vignette, low apparent resolution.
- Excels: detached observation and eerie empty spaces.
- Failure character: bright sources contaminate the frame and shadows become electronic.

## Pocket Compact (`point-shoot`)

- Protects: face exposure, small-sensor clarity, and clean flash color.
- Sacrifices: shallow-depth subtlety and ambient shadow mood.
- Exposure: confident auto-everything response.
- Color: glossy but more disciplined than Pocket 2002.
- Grain/optics: clean digital noise, modest corner weakness.
- Excels: everyday immediacy and travel.
- Failure character: mixed lighting may look computational rather than atmospheric.

## Pastel Cinema (`pastel-cinema`)

- Protects: pale color relationships and gentle highlight separation.
- Sacrifices: deep blacks and hard local contrast.
- Exposure: high-key friendly with softly opened shadows.
- Color: reduced saturation with selective pastel preservation.
- Grain/optics: fine grain and mild bloom.
- Excels: arranged scenes and soft daylight.
- Failure character: already flat scenes can become deliberately delicate.

## Neon Night (`tokyo-neon`)

- Protects: meaningful colored light and dark atmosphere.
- Sacrifices: neutral white balance and broad shadow visibility.
- Exposure: meters low so signs and practical lights retain color.
- Color: preserves cyan, magenta, and mixed illuminants instead of neutralising them.
- Grain/optics: colored bloom, modest fringe, dark corners.
- Excels: wet streets, signage, and nightlife.
- Failure character: subjects outside colored light may remain obscure.

## Photobooth (`photobooth`)

- Protects: face placement, graphic skin tone, and strip-like directness.
- Sacrifices: color, soft ambience, and environmental detail.
- Exposure: hard frontal flash centered on faces.
- Color: strictly monochrome.
- Grain/optics: moderate grain and close-focus softness.
- Excels: one or two close faces.
- Failure character: distant groups flatten and backgrounds disappear.

## Wet Plate (`tintype`)

- Protects: portrait presence and broad facial planes.
- Sacrifices: modern color, clean borders, and quick-exposure perfection.
- Exposure: conservative plate-like response with deep edge loss.
- Color: cool silver orthochromatic near-monochrome — reds run dark, blues run pale (owner-directed collodion plate, not warm sepia).
- Grain/optics: coarse plate texture, strong vignette, uneven edges.
- Excels: still portraits and simple backgrounds.
- Failure character: movement and busy scenes become heavy and strange.
