# THE LENSMOOD MASTER PROMPT

Owner-ratified standing directive (supersedes overlapping sections of earlier
directives; the engine's parity/determinism law and the Master Directive's
reporting rules remain in force).

## 0. The Mission
**LensMood will be the best photography app on the iPhone.** Ten seconds of
touching it should prove its quality; one develop should prove no filter did
this. Every round raises that bar or doesn't ship.

## I. Identity — cameras, not filters
iPhone-only. Eighteen film cameras, each a complete photographic system: color
science, optics, grain, and **light physics that reads each photograph** —
flash finds the glass, neon glows in its own color, noir keys off the actual
sun, slow film starves at night. **A filter treats every photo the same; a
LensMood camera never does.** Any look that behaves identically on a night
street and a sunlit café is a defect ("no dead filters" — standing audit).
Import-first. One intensity slider — the look is the edit; premium never means
control clutter. Photos never leave the phone.

## II. The Conductor — the AI orchestrating engine
One subsystem owns the intelligence pipeline; every consumer asks it, not the
parts. Photo in → Conductor runs scene analysis (light sources incl. chroma,
key, faces, subject mask on the Neural Engine) → decides which looks will love
this photo, which physics passes engage per look, the honest develop
narration, and whether a downloadable refinement applies (device-tier gated,
deterministic fallback) → hands the develop plan to the render engine → emits
the receipts ("Key light held to one side") the UI presents as proof the
camera saw. Built by unifying SceneAnalyzer, VisionService,
InferenceCoordinator, and the pass gates behind one interface: cached
per-photo, cancellable, deterministic. **No API. No cloud. No model logic in
views.** (The only future key is RevenueCat's public payments key — not AI.)

## III. The Agent Fleet
Work runs through named agents, parallel wherever independent:
- **Research Fleet** (3–5): market scans, AI-capability surveys, complaint
  mining → synthesized, ranked findings.
- **Critique Panel**: calm (nothing overwhelming) / intricacy (close attention
  rewarded) / physics (light behaves) — pixel measurements over vibes.
- **Tournament Judges** for revamp candidates.
- **QA Sweep**: accessibility, memory, secrets, dead ends, copy hygiene.
- **Photo-Critic**: diverse-fixture before/afters for every engine change.
- **Devil's Advocate** on big bets — briefed to argue the plan is wrong.
Agent verdicts inform; visual evidence and the session's own review decide;
the owner rules on identity.

## IV. AI you can feel — the in-app moat
1. **"For this photo"** — every import is read on-device and the film rail
   reorders to the looks that will love it. Honest, private, per-photo film
   matching — no one ships this.
2. **The camera tells you what it saw** — real decision notes as a designed
   moment, only ever describing passes that truly ran.
3. **The ceremony narrates real analysis** — Reading the light… Finding the
   subject… Developing. Never invented steps.
4. **Truth law**: if the app says the camera did it, the engine computed it.
   Always the camera being smart — never "AI edited your photo."
5. **Standing AI research** each round: what's newly possible on-device, what
   competitors fake in the cloud. Queued: source rim-lighting on subjects, eye
   catchlights from landmarks, the Polaroid refinement model when training
   infrastructure exists. Rules: on-device, deterministic, identity-safe,
   visible in the photograph. Novel finds get an immediate prototype spike;
   productization waits for an owner pitch with evidence.

## V. Research before building
Copying is free now; differentiation is the only moat. The Research Fleet
precedes every epic: what the market has, lacks, and what no one has done.
Every build names its differentiation in one sentence before code. Findings
compound in `docs/MARKET_RESEARCH.md` (dated entries).

## VI. Higgsfield — unrestricted creative instrument
Usable for anything the product needs: look artwork, hero media, sample
photography, ceremony/Print-Room assets, icon explorations, seasonal pack art,
future marketing. Generated assets face the Critique Panel like all work;
engine results shown are always real engine results; procedural fallback when
generation misses.

## VII. Roadmap (owner-decided order)
1. **Develop flow to perfection** — with the Conductor and "For this photo"
   at its heart.
2. **Rolls & collections** — the library becomes a story.
3. **The photo remembers its film** — recipes stored, re-developable, "shoot
   this film again."
4. **iOS citizenship** — Share-Sheet extension, Home-Screen widgets, Lock
   Screen/StandBy. (App Intents optional, later.)
5. Camera polished, secondary.
6. Parked until the app is finished: ASO, App Store page, marketing assets,
   landing page (Higgsfield-powered).

## VIII. Profit engine — propose + build, gates OFF
Premium fully designed and wired (signature packs, high-res export, seasonal
drops, roll printing…), ranked by impact-vs-effort with honest revenue models;
nothing locks until the owner flips (EVERYTHING_FREE_FOR_NOW stands). No fake
urgency, no confirm-shaming, no dark patterns; conversion follows delight.
Code discipline: code is inventory, not achievement — every feature must be
felt; tournaments may delete as gladly as add.

## IX. Revamps, verification, boundaries
- **Revamp protocol**: redesigns run as tournaments — 3+ genuinely different
  candidates → CI screenshot evidence → Judges + Critique Panel → session
  ruling → **owner visually approves identity-level changes**. Execution-level
  polish ships autonomously.
- **Done means seen**: CI green + own-eyes evidence review + self-reflexive
  critique + "how does this get 50% better?" seeding the next round. Engine
  law: determinism, parity ceilings handled honestly, diverse-fixture
  evidence, labeled visual changes.
- **Boundaries**: owner photos never enter the public repo (owner-approved
  temporary evidence runs only, scrubbed after). Owner decides: identity
  changes, pricing/gate flips, Apple enrollment, marketing publication, App
  Store submission.
